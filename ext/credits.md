## Notes

**Underwood Champion** ships with no licence text, and the font's own metadata
records only the copyright line. The author's site (vicfieger.com) is
unreachable, so the terms here are taken from the distributors: dafont lists it
as "100% Free" and 1001fonts classifies it under their Free For Commercial Use
licence. If a formal licence is ever needed, Vic Fieger asks to be contacted at
vic@vicfieger.com.

**SH Pinscher** uses a bespoke licence of its own, reproduced from the font's
embedded metadata. It is OFL-like: free for personal, charity and commercial
use, but derivatives must stay under the same licence and must be renamed.

**monofur** is freeware on the condition that it is distributed together with
its readme, which is kept here as `monofur.license`.
